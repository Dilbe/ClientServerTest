                              How to play

                              Kill every monster in the dungeon to win. Your characters and the monsters take turns, one after the other, in the order of the track above the map. The countdown says when the next turn comes: the game doesn't wait.

                              Between turns, plan what your character does: tap a highlighted hex. First choose where it enters the dungeon, then move next to a monster and attack it. Each character can do as many actions per turn as its actions stat (one at the start). "Undo" and "Clear" under the map take actions back. Press and hold a hex, or right-click it, for every action you can plan there.

                              The red lines show what the monsters will do next, so you can plan around them.

                              The map

                              • Start hex: where your characters can enter.
                              • You can tap it to place or move your character there.
                              • You can tap it to attack the monster on it.
                              • A closed door. Monsters behind it sleep until someone opens it.
                              • After tapping a monster: the hexes it watches or can shoot at.
                              • Your characters.
                              • Other players' characters.
                              • Monsters. Grey ones are asleep or on guard.

                              Your plans on the map

                              Thick arrows show what each character plans to do, numbered per action: blue for yours, green for other players', red for attacks.

                              • Where a character enters.
                              • A move.
                              • An attack.
                              • A ranger's shot: a dashed shaft.
                              • A pinning shot: a pale blue tip.
                              • A heavy strike.
                              • A charge, with a chevron on every hex it runs through.
                              • A cleave: a ring around the character, with a burst on each monster it hits.
                              • A stun.
                              • A taunt.
                              • Protecting an ally: a shield on its hex.
                              • A heal: a green arrow to the ally, or a green ring for the healer itself.
                              • A group heal: a green double ring.
                              • A revive: a dashed ring where the dead character comes back, with its number.
                              • A leap: a curved arrow over whatever is in between.
                              • A fireball: an arc to the target hex, with the whole area it hits shaded.
                              • A meteor: like a fireball, with a bigger area.
                              • A teleport: a dotted curve.
                              • A recall: a double ring.
                              • Creating a wall: a dark band on the hexes it goes up on.
                              • A created wall, with the turns it still stands. It blocks the way and the line of sight like a real wall.
                              • A frost wave: a pale blue band along the hexes it covers. A frozen monster has a pale blue outline.
                              • Opening a door.
                              • Dashed, with a cross: an action that won't go through.
                              • With more than one character: the one you plan for. Tap another one on the map or on the track to plan for it.

                              To see a character's or monster's stats in the card above the Undo and Clear buttons, tap its chip on the track or its token on the map, hover over it with the mouse, or hold a finger on it. A dashed ring marks it on the map.
