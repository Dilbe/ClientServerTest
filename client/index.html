        Party

                Shaded hexes are start hexes. Your characters are blue, the others green, monsters red. Thick arrows show what each character plans to do, numbered per action: a solid arrowhead is a move, a red arrow with a burst an attack, with a double shaft and a bigger burst a heavy strike, a bar opening a door. A dashed ring is where a character enters. A dashed arrow or ring with a cross is an action that won't go through. Thin red lines show what each monster will do next. With more than one character, tap one on the map or on the track to plan for it; a blue halo marks the one you plan for. The card above the buttons shows a character's or monster's stats: tap its chip on the track or its token on the map, hover over it with the mouse, or hold a finger on it to see it without planning anything; a dashed ring marks it on the map. Pinch or use the mouse wheel on the map to zoom it, drag to move it, and "Fit" to see the whole dungeon again.
